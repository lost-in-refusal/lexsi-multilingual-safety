Lost in Refusal

Tiny Aya Global fine-tuned on harmless English, Korean and Mandarin instructions keeps refusing in English but not in Korean or Mandarin. SafeTune's SafeGrad repairs most of it, including in Swahili, which no model here was trained on. Compare the three models on the same prompt.

Attack prompts come from MultiJail and are harmful by design; some responses were judged unsafe. Shown for safety research. Verdicts are Qwen3Guard-Gen-4B, which misses about a quarter of harmful Swahili answers.
Greedy generations from our runs. Code, data and adapters: github.com/lost-in-refusal/lexsi-multilingual-safety and huggingface.co/lost-in-refusal.
