# Paper (EMNLP / ACL format)

Style files: `acl.sty` and `acl_natbib.bst` from github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files (the template EMNLP uses).

Build from this folder:

    latexmk -pdf main.tex

Figures are read straight from `results/lir/` and `results/lir/stage2/`, so a regenerated figure updates the paper.

Limits from the organizers: under 6 pages of main text, up to 6 pages of appendix. Limitations, ethics,
references and appendix are outside the main-text count. Overleaf: zip this folder plus the figures and upload.
